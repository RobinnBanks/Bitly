The logged‑in home page should feel like a real, functioning app. It includes:

1. Bitly Buddy Status Panel (Main Focus)
The buddy is front and center. It reacts to the user’s day using safe, hypothetical diabetes patterns.

The buddy shows:

Energy level

Mood

Hunger level

Hydration level

Activity level

Glucose trend (friendly, non‑medical)

Examples of buddy reactions:

Stable glucose:  
The buddy looks energetic, bouncy, sparkly.

Glucose trending high:  
The buddy looks warm, slow, and refuses food:
“Your Bitly doesn’t need sugar right now.”

Glucose trending low:  
The buddy looks tired or droopy:
“Your Bitly might need a snack soon.”

After eating a meal:  
The buddy reacts differently depending on the food type:

Salad → gentle energy boost

Granola bar → medium boost

Egg → stable boost

Sugary snack → quick spike, then slow down
The buddy says things like:
“That helped! I feel steadier now.”  
or
“That was a lot of sugar. I’m full.”

If the user tries to feed the buddy when glucose is high:  
The buddy refuses:
“I can’t eat right now. Let’s wait a bit.”

If the user hasn’t eaten in hours:  
The buddy rubs its belly:
“I’m getting hungry. Maybe a snack?”

If the user logs activity (games, walking, chores):  
The buddy reacts:
“Fun! That helped my energy.”  
or
“Games were fun! Want to log more activity?”

This makes the buddy feel alive and connected to the user’s real patterns.

🎯 3. Daily Goals (Parent or Teen Mode)
Teen Mode
Teens can set:

Meal goals

Water goals

Activity goals

“Check glucose” goals

Progress bars fill as they log things.

Parent Mode
Parents can set goals for their child:

“Eat 3 meals today”

“Drink 5 glasses of water”

“Check glucose 4 times”

Parents can also send reminders:

“Your buddy looks hungry — time for a snack?”

“Your buddy wants water.”

“Your buddy wants to check in.”

🔔 4. Buddy‑Based Notifications (Interactive Demo)
Notifications come from the buddy, not the app.

Examples:

“I’m hungry — want to log a snack?”

“I’m thirsty — water time?”

“I’m feeling low energy — maybe check your glucose?”

“I’m full — no more sugar right now.”

Parents can send notifications too:

“Your buddy needs a snack.”

“Your buddy wants to check in.”

⚙️ 5. Settings (Fully Interactive)
From the logged‑in view, users can:

Change theme (Bubble Yellow, Bubble Pink, Bubble Mix, Dark, Blue)

Customize buddy (hair, skin tone, clothes, accessories)

Change email

Change password

Enable 2‑step verification

Turn buddy notifications on/off

Adjust accessibility:

High contrast

Larger text

Dyslexia‑friendly font

Reduce animations

Log out

When logged out, the app returns to the public home page.

🎮 6. Activity Log (New Section You Requested)

Users can log:

Games played

Walking

Sports

Chores

School activities

The buddy reacts:

“Games were fun!”

“Walking helped my energy.”

“I’m tired after all that activity.”

This becomes part of the buddy’s daily mood.

🖼️ 7. Buddy & Image Loading (Online Assets)
All buddy animations, icons, and images should load instantly.

If something fails:

Show a fallback buddy

Message: “Reloading your buddy…”

Buddy shows an “Online” indicator when synced.
