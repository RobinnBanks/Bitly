Bitly must support:
- Parent accounts
- Teen accounts (self-managed)
- Kid accounts (created by parents)

LOGIN & LOGOUT BEHAVIOR
- When logged out, show:
  - Logo, tagline
  - "Log in" and "Sign up" buttons
  - "About Bitly" section
  - Account type choices: Parent / Teen / Kid (with a message: "If you are a kid, please get your parent to create your account.")
- When logged in:
  - Hide "About Us", "Log in", and "Sign up" from navigation.
  - Show "Home", "Settings", "Notifications", "Log out".
  - Display "Logged in as Parent" or "Logged in as Teen".
  - Remember the user's email (e.g., Gmail) for future logins.
- When logging out:
  - End the session.
  - Return to the public home page with "About Bitly", "Log in", and "Sign up" visible again.

HOME PAGE (LOGGED-IN)
- Show a "Glucose monitor status" card:
  - For the demo, always show "Glucose monitor: Online" with a green dot.
  - Show last sync time and a small smiling buddy icon.
- Show the Tamagotchi buddy panel:
  - Pixelated character with current mood (happy, hungry, tired, etc.).
  - Text like: "Your buddy looks a little hungry. Have you eaten?"
  - Buttons:
    - For parents: "Send reminder".
    - For teens: "Set my own reminder".
- Show a "Daily goals" card:
  - For parents: "Set today’s goals for your child" (meals, water, glucose checks).
  - For teens: "Set your goals for today" (same fields, self-managed).
  - Progress bars that fill as logs are completed.
- Show quick actions:
  - "Go to Settings"
  - "View Today’s Chart"
  - "Send a reminder"
  - "Check Tamagotchi buddy"

NOTIFICATIONS & BUDDY INTERACTIONS
- Parents can send reminders to their kids:
  - Example messages:
    - "Your Bitly buddy looks hungry—time for a snack?"
    - "Your buddy is low on energy—maybe check your glucose?"
    - "Water time! Your buddy wants a drink."
- Automatic buddy-based notifications (demo logic):
  - If no meal logged in 4 hours → "Your buddy hasn’t eaten in a while. Want to log a snack?"
  - If no water logged in 3 hours → "Your buddy is thirsty. Log some water?"
  - If glucose monitor is online but no reading logged → "Your buddy wants to check in. Log your latest reading?"
- Include a "Notifications" tab where users can see past reminders and mark them as done.

SETTINGS, ACCESSIBILITY, AND THEMES
- Settings must include:
  - Change email
  - Change password
  - Enable/disable 2-step verification
  - Theme selection:
    - Bubble Yellow (default)
    - Bubble Pink
    - Bubble Mix (yellow + pink)
    - Dark mode
    - Blue mode
  - Tamagotchi buddy customization (hair, skin tone, clothes, accessories).
  - Accessibility options:
    - High contrast mode
    - Larger text
    - Dyslexia-friendly font
    - Reduce animations.
  - Notification preferences:
    - Turn buddy notifications on/off.
    - Choose tone: "Gentle", "Playful", "Minimal".
  - Log out button.
- Accessibility behavior:
  - High contrast mode increases contrast.
  - Reduce animations mode minimizes motion and sparkles.
  - Buttons are large and easy to tap.

BUDDIES & IMAGES ONLINE
- Show an "Online" indicator next to the buddy when the app is connected.
- If assets fail to load, show a fallback buddy with a message like:
  - "We’re reloading your buddy—please wait."
- All character images, icons, and charts should load quickly with soft, pastel loading animations.

Design language:
- Soft pastels (bubble yellow, pink, mix, dark, blue).
- Rounded corners, bubbly shapes.
- Pixelated Tamagotchi-style buddy.
- Friendly, non-clinical tone.
- Fully interactive login, home, settings, notifications, and goal-setting flows for parents and teens.
